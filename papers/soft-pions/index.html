Are hybrid hydrodynamic models missing soft-pion physics?

O. Ferreira, T. S. Domingues, F. G. Gardim, et al. · arXiv:2611.18290 (2026)

🇧🇷 Versão em português

Hybrid models, in which relativistic viscous hydrodynamics is followed by a hadronic afterburner, are the standard tool for describing the bulk evolution of heavy-ion collisions. We performed a systematic Bayesian analysis of the full parameter space of a state-of-the-art hybrid model and found that no combination of parameters reproduces the pion spectra measured by ALICE at low transverse momentum: the data show an excess of soft pions, while no comparable excess appears in the spectra of kaons, protons or antiprotons.

The excess persists when the hadronic afterburner is included, and the scaled pion spectra keep their universal shape across centralities. Adding a one-parameter enhancement mechanism to the Bayesian analysis, we found that the data favor mechanisms that act mainly at low pT and fade at high pT. This points to non-equilibrium effects that are missing from current hybrid simulations.
